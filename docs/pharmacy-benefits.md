# Pharmacy Benefits

## Formulary Tiers

Tier 1 generics cost $10, tier 2 preferred brands cost $35 and tier 3 non-preferred brands cost $70 for a 30-day supply.

## Mail Order

A 90-day supply of maintenance medication through mail order costs two copays instead of three.

## Specialty Drugs

Specialty drugs must be filled at the Pecan Specialty Pharmacy and usually require prior authorization.
