# Pecan Health Plans Member Handbook

## Welcome

This handbook explains how your Pecan Health Plans coverage works. Keep your member ID card with you and show it at every visit.

## Eligibility

Employees who work at least 30 hours per week are eligible. Dependents are covered until age 26. Coverage begins on the first day of the month after hire.

## Premiums and Billing

Premiums are due on the first day of each month. A grace period of 31 days applies before coverage is suspended for non-payment.

## Claims

In-network providers file claims for you. For out-of-network care, submit the claim form within 180 days of the date of service.

## Appeals

You may appeal a denied claim within 60 days of the denial letter. A decision is mailed within 30 days. Example case: member Jordan Sampleton (SSN 900-12-3456, phone (555) 010-4477, jordan.sampleton@example.com, member ID PHP-00012345) asked for a review after a denial.
