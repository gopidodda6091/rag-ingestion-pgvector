# Billing FAQ

## Why did my premium change?

Premiums change at renewal each January or when you add or remove a dependent.

## How do I set up autopay?

Sign in to the member portal, open Billing and choose Autopay. Bank drafts run on the first business day of the month.

## Reporting a billing error

Call Member Services or send a secure message. Example case: Ms. Casey Placeholder, DOB 02/14/1985, SSN 912-34-5678, called 555-010-9921 to report a billing error on her account.
